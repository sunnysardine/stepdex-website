The session

Understand the issue.
Choose the right STEP.

Performance challenges often appear as missed deadlines, repeated mistakes or poor cooperation. This session helps leaders look beneath those symptoms and choose development actions that address the underlying conditions.

01 / Diagnose

Look beyond attitude.

Explore six performance conditions: information, resources, incentives, knowledge and skills, capacity, and motivation. Use real management situations to examine the root cause.

02 / Develop

See leadership as a system.

Use the STEPDEX® dimensions of Strategy, Technical, Empower and People to discuss capability gaps and create a clearer development path.

03 / Apply

Turn insight into action.

Work through practical diagnostic and management tools. Connect leadership development to the challenges your organisation needs to address.
